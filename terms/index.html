What Elderly does

Elderly places friendly phone calls to an older adult you name, on a schedule you choose, and sends you a short written note after each call. The calls are held by an artificial voice, not by a person.

Elderly is not a medical service and not an emergency service. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition. It cannot call 911, and no one is watching the calls in real time. If someone may be in danger, call emergency services yourself.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and live in the United States. You may only sign up an older adult if you have a genuine family or caregiving relationship with them, and you must be able to tell them honestly that these calls are coming.

The permission that matters most

Before we keep recordings of anyone, the first call asks the older adult, out loud, whether they are alright with being called and recorded and with a note going to their family. If they say no, the account stops there. You may not use Elderly to call someone who has refused, or to call someone under a false pretense.

Many states require every party to a recorded call to consent. That is why we ask. Do not ask us to work around it.
