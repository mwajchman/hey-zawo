Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Elderly calls an older adult you love, and sends you a short note afterward. That means we handle recordings of real conversations with someone who may not be the person paying us. This page explains exactly what we keep, who can see it, and how to make it go away.

Who we are

Elderly is operated by Zawo LLC, formerly named TalentLink LLC — the same company, renamed with the State of Utah on May 11, 2026. You can reach a human at matias@callelderly.com. Elderly is available in the United States only.

The two people in every account

Almost every account involves two people, and they are not treated the same way:

  • The family member who signs up, pays, and reads the notes. That is the account holder.
  • The older adult who receives the calls. They are not our customer, but the conversation is theirs. We ask for their permission out loud, on the first call, before we keep any recording.

What we collect

From the family member

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Password (stored only as a one-way hash) or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account email and name. We never receive your Google password.
  • What you tell us about the older adult when setting up: their name, phone number, time zone, preferred call times, and any context you want the calls to include (interests, family names, health topics to avoid).
  • Requests you make, such as asking us to bring up a specific memory or question on an upcoming call.
  • Payment records. Card numbers are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers.

From the calls

  • An audio recording of each call, and a written transcript of it.
  • Call metadata: date, time, length, whether the call was answered, and by whom (person or voicemail).
  • A short written summary of each call, generated automatically from the transcript, plus flags for things worth your attention (for example: they mentioned pain, or they sounded down).

From the website

  • Basic usage analytics on our public pages, so we know which pages people read. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share data for advertising.

Consent from the older adult

On the very first call, before anything is stored as part of their profile, the caller says plainly that the calls are recorded and that a note goes to their family, and asks whether that is alright. Their answer is recorded and kept as part of the account.

If they say no, the account cannot proceed. We do not keep calling someone who declined, and we do not deliver notes about them. The family member is told.

Either person can withdraw that permission later. The older adult can say so on any call, or anyone can email us. We stop within one business day.

How we use it

  • To place the calls, hold the conversation, and write the note you receive afterward.
  • To let the calls remember earlier conversations, so the older adult is not asked the same thing every day.
  • To alert you when something in a call warrants attention.
  • To send you a text message after each call with a link to that call's note.
  • To bill you, support you, and keep the service running and secure.

We do not sell personal information, share it with data brokers, or use call content to advertise anything to anyone. We do not use your recordings or transcripts to train our own general-purpose AI models.

Text messages

We text the account holder, at the mobile number they entered themselves and confirmed with a one-time code. Two kinds of message, and nothing else:

  • A verification code when you add or change your number.
  • A short note after each completed call, with a link that opens that call's summary inside your portal.

Message frequency: one message per completed call, up to seven per week depending on the schedule you chose, plus the occasional verification code.

We never send marketing or promotional texts. Every message we send is transactional: a code you asked for, or a note about a call that just happened. There is no marketing consent bundled into signing up, because there is no marketing.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them, or HELP for help. Stopping the texts does not stop the calls or close your account.

We do not share mobile numbers. No mobile information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Phone numbers reach our messaging provider only to deliver the messages described above, and that provider may not use them for anything else.

Who in your family can see what

The account holder can invite other family members. Invited people see the notes and call history for the older adults they were given access to, and nothing else. The account holder can remove anyone's access at any time, and removal takes effect immediately.

How long we keep it

  • Recordings and transcripts: kept while the account is active, so the calls can remember. Deleted within 30 days of account closure.
  • Notes and summaries: the same.
  • Billing records: kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years.
  • Consent recordings: kept for as long as we called that person, plus one year, because they are the record that we had permission.

Your rights

Wherever you live in the United States, and regardless of whether your state has a privacy law, we will honor these requests:

  • See it. Ask us for a copy of everything we hold about you or your family member.
  • Correct it. Fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete it. Close the account and have the data erased. You can do this from your portal, or by emailing us.
  • Stop the texts. Reply STOP to any message, or turn them off in your portal.

Write to matias@callelderly.com and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you or degrade your service for asking.

If you are the older adult being called, these rights are yours too, and you do not need the account holder's permission to use them.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to the production database is limited to the people who operate the service. Recordings are never public: they are served only to a signed-in account, and the link alone is not enough to open one. The database is backed up automatically several times an hour.

No system is perfect. If we ever have a breach that affects you, we will tell you what happened, what was exposed, and what we are doing about it.

Children

Elderly is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change something that matters, we will email the account holder before it takes effect, and update the date at the top of this page.
