❤️ Health check-ins — the science of early signals Not in {her} plan
Voice, sleep and routine drift measurably weeks before a doctor's visit catches decline — slower speech, shorter answers, meals skipped, nights interrupted. Daily check-in calls are a validated tool in heart-failure care and post-fall recovery for exactly this reason.
